# Privacy Policy for Pomo Social

**Last updated:** September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pomo Social ("**Pomo Social**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our iOS application (the "**App**").

Pomo Social is operated by Alain Hamilton, an individual, based in Kentucky, USA. For any questions about this policy, contact us at **pomosocialapp@gmail.com**.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you provide
- **Account information:** When you sign up using Sign in with Apple, we receive a unique identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address.
- **Profile information:** The handle (username) you choose, and any avatar/profile settings you select.
- **Content you create:** Goals and goal categories you set, group names you create, friend requests, and reports you file against other users.
- **Photos (planned feature):** If and when photo-accountability is enabled, photos you choose to submit after completing a pomodoro session.

### Information collected automatically
- **Usage and activity data:** Number of pomodoros completed, session timestamps, streaks, and career totals, which are stored to power your profile stats and friend/group features.
- **Device information:** Basic technical data (such as device type and OS version) that our infrastructure provider (Firebase) may log for security and reliability purposes.

We do **not** currently use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If we add any in the future (for example, crash reporting or product analytics), we will update this policy before doing so.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and maintain your account and handle
- Operate core features: pomodoro timers, streaks, goals, friends, groups, and leaderboards
- Show your friends that you completed a pomodoro (subject to our anti-addiction stat-lock design, described below)
- Investigate reports and enforce blocking between users
- Maintain the security and integrity of the App
- Communicate with you about the service, if necessary

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. How Information Is Shared

- **Other users:** Your handle, avatar, pomodoro activity (whether you completed a session), and — once you've unlocked stats for the day — your counts, are visible to friends and group members you connect with. You control your friend list and group memberships, and can block or report other users at any time.
- **Service providers:** We use **Firebase** (a Google service) for authentication, database (Firestore), and — for the planned photo feature — file storage (Cloud Storage). Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google's own security and privacy commitments.
- **Legal reasons:** We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Pomo Social, our users, or the public.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing **pomosocialapp@gmail.com**. We will delete your data within a reasonable time, except where retention is required for legal or security purposes (for example, records related to reports of abuse).

## 5. Your Choices

- You can edit or delete goals, leave groups, unfriend users, and block/report other users directly in the App.
- You can request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us.
- Because Pomo Social deliberately avoids social/leaderboard push notifications, the only notification you'll receive is a local timer-completion alert, which you can disable in your device's notification settings.

## 6. Age Requirement

Pomo Social is not directed to, and may not be used by, anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe someone under 18 has used the App, please contact us at **pomosocialapp@gmail.com**.

## 7. Security

We use commercially reasonable measures, including Firebase's security infrastructure and access-control rules, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. International Users

Pomo Social is operated from the United States. If you use the App from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email **pomosocialapp@gmail.com**.
